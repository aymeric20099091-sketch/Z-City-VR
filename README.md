# Z-City VR

**A complete, VR-exclusive adaptation of Z-City for Garry's Mod.**

Bringing Z-City's physics-driven gameplay, ragdolls, medical systems, and immersive interactions into virtual reality.

**Status:** 🟡 Planning & Early Development

**Platform:** Garry's Mod (PCVR)

**License:** GNU AGPL-3.0

---

## About the Project

**Z-City VR** is an open-source project aiming to transform the original Z-City gamemode into a fully VR-native experience.

Z-City is known for its physics-based gameplay, detailed medical and injury systems, ragdoll mechanics, and unpredictable player interactions.

The goal isn't simply to make Z-City playable with a VR headset.

**The goal is to rebuild its interactions and physical mechanics around virtual reality while preserving everything that makes Z-City unique.**

Rather than creating another traditional Z-City server, this project aims to explore what Z-City could become when players physically interact with its world.

### Our Vision

- Preserve Z-City's core gameplay and identity.
- Rework existing mechanics to take advantage of VR.
- Create an immersive, physics-driven multiplayer experience.
- Support VR players exclusively, without compromising the experience for traditional PC controls.
- Keep the project open source and accessible to contributors.

## Planned Features

The following features represent the project's long-term goals. **They are not implemented yet.**

### Full-Body VR Interaction

- Full-body player representation.
- Head and hand tracking.
- Physics-aware body movement.
- Natural interactions with the environment.
- Body positioning and movement adapted to VR.

### Physics & Ragdolls

One of Z-City's defining features is its physics system.

Z-City VR aims to adapt this system to accommodate tracked VR movements.

Planned improvements include:

- VR-compatible ragdoll mechanics.
- Physical reactions to environmental interactions.
- Smooth transitions between player control and ragdoll states.
- Physics-driven character interactions.
- Body synchronization between VR players.

### Medical & Injury Systems

Z-City's medical mechanics are a central part of its gameplay.

Rather than replacing them, the project aims to preserve and adapt them for VR.

Planned features include:

- VR-native medical interactions.
- Physical interactions with medical equipment.
- Adaptation of the existing injury and health systems.
- Interactive treatment mechanics.
- Immersive feedback for player conditions.

The objective is to make medical gameplay feel natural in VR while retaining the complexity of the original systems.

### VR-Native Interactions

Traditional keyboard and mouse interactions will need to be redesigned.

The project aims to support:

- Physical object manipulation.
- VR-compatible item handling.
- Hand-based world interactions.
- Redesigned menus and interfaces.
- Natural interactions between players.

### VR-Exclusive Multiplayer

Z-City VR is designed specifically for VR players.

- VR headset required to participate.
- Multiplayer synchronization of tracked movements.
- VR-oriented gameplay mechanics.
- Server-side VR session validation.
- Multiplayer physics synchronization.

Traditional desktop gameplay is not a development target.

## Development Roadmap

Development will be divided into several phases.

### Phase 1 — Research & Foundation

- [ ] Analyze the original Z-City codebase.
- [ ] Evaluate existing Garry's Mod VR frameworks.
- [ ] Identify major compatibility issues.
- [ ] Establish the project's development structure.
- [ ] Create an initial VR testing environment.

### Phase 2 — VR Player System

- [ ] Implement VR player tracking.
- [ ] Establish full-body representation.
- [ ] Adapt player movement.
- [ ] Synchronize VR movements in multiplayer.
- [ ] Implement VR-only player validation.

### Phase 3 — Physics & Ragdolls

- [ ] Investigate Z-City's existing ragdoll implementation.
- [ ] Adapt ragdoll behavior for VR.
- [ ] Implement transitions between controlled and physics-driven states.
- [ ] Improve physical player interactions.
- [ ] Test multiplayer physics behavior.

### Phase 4 — Gameplay Integration

- [ ] Adapt the existing medical systems.
- [ ] Implement VR medical interactions.
- [ ] Adapt item and inventory interactions.
- [ ] Rework relevant gameplay controls.
- [ ] Integrate VR-compatible interfaces.

### Phase 5 — Multiplayer Testing & Optimization

- [ ] Test interactions between multiple VR players.
- [ ] Identify and fix synchronization issues.
- [ ] Optimize physics and networking performance.
- [ ] Improve VR comfort and usability.
- [ ] Conduct extended gameplay testing.

### Phase 6 — Public Testing

- [ ] Prepare a playable testing build.
- [ ] Organize community playtests.
- [ ] Collect feedback and bug reports.
- [ ] Improve stability and compatibility.
- [ ] Prepare the first public release.

**This roadmap is provisional and may change as technical limitations and development priorities become clearer.**

## Current Development Status

Z-City VR is currently in its initial planning stage.

No playable build is available yet.

Progress will be documented through this repository.
